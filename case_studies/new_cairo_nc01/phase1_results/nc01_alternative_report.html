11. Assumptions, limitations and uncertainty

  • Air temperature is not land surface temperature and neither alone measures pedestrian thermal comfort.
  • Flat terrain assumed; surrounding off-site buildings and terrain are not modelled.
  • Indicators describe the proposed geometry as supplied. They do not predict temperatures or comfort.
  • Land SURFACE temperature at ~10:30 local solar time on clear days; not air temperature or pedestrian thermal comfort.
  • NDVI indicates vegetation-related spectral characteristics; it does not measure tree health, shade quality, irrigation or cooling.
  • OSM tagging and coverage vary by place; mapped land use is not verified ground truth.
  • Observed conditions describe the existing site, not the proposed design.
  • Observed vegetation describes current conditions at the image dates, not the proposed design.
  • Relative classes compare pixels within this study area only; they do not establish a causal link between any building and heating.
  • Results are labelled as geometric checks against user-verified rules; URBANOVA does not grant statutory approval.
  • Retrieved on Open-Meteo's free tier, which permits non-commercial use only. Commercial use of URBANOVA requires an Open-Meteo subscription or another source.
  • Thermal band native resolution is 100 m, so features smaller than ~100 m are not resolved even on the 30 m grid.
  • Times are true solar time (12:00 = sun due south/north), not clock time.
  • Trees and shade structures are treated as opaque; real canopies transmit some light.
  • Values come from Open-Meteo Historical Weather API (ERA5 reanalysis) gridded reanalysis at ERA5 0.25° (~25 km) grid. They describe regional conditions, not the microclimate inside the development.
  • Findings marked PROVISIONAL THRESHOLD use severity bands that have not been approved by the AI/Analysis team; they are not standards or legal limits.
  • Unknown, unavailable, blocked and not-applicable values are shown as such and never as zero.
